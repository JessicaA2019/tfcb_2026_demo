## Procedures
Day 1 - Seed (4) 25cm2 flasks with 250,000 cells of neff. Let grow to 70-80% confluency over night

Day 2
1. Check cell condition
2. Make 50mL of PYG (+Amp/Kan/Chlor).
3. Aspirate off old media and wash with 4mL of fresh media.
4. Add 4sU to 5 mL of fresh media to make following concentrations in 5mL tubes. 
	a. 100uM - add ___uL 4sU
	b. 250uM - add ___uL 4sU
	c. 500uM - add ___uL 4sU
	d. 1000uM - add ___uL 4sU
5. Invert tubes to mix 4sU.
6. Record pre-treatment cell condition.
7. Aspirate the wash media from the plate. Add the culture medium containing 4sU to the cells. 
	a. avoid any UV light as 4sU will crosslink.
8. Incubate cells with 4sU culture medium covered from light.
9. Record cell status at 30, 60, 90, and 120 minutes post-4sU addition.
10. For 2 surviving time points, aspirate the 4sU culture medium and 1mL of Monarch StabiLyse Buffer and freeze flask.

Day 3
1. Finish RNA isolation spin kit
	a. Do optional on column DNA digestion
	b. Elute in 50uL RNAse-free water

Day 4
Biotinylation:
1. Labeling Reaction (use 100 μg total RNA):
	a. 200 μl Biotin-HPDP (1mg/ml DMF) 
	b. 100 μl 10X Biotinylation Buffer
	c. bring up to 700 μl with RNase-free water 
2. Rotate at room temperature in the dark for at least 1.5 hr.
3. Add an equal volume of Phenol/Chloroform pH 6.7.
4. Mix vigorously by vortex or by manually shaking.
5. Incubate for 2 – 3 minutes at room temperature until phases begin to separate and bubbles start to disappear.
6. Centrifuge at full speed (20,000 × g) for 5 min at room temp.
7. Carefully transfer upper phase into new tubes.
8. RNA precipitation: Add 70uL of 5 M NaCl.
9. Add an equal volume of isopropanol, invert to mix well.
10. Centrifuge at 20,000 × g for 20 min.
11. Remove supernatant. Add an equal volume of 75 % EtOH.
12. Centrifuge at 20,000 × g for 10 min.
13. Remove EtOH completely and re-suspend the RNA pellet at approximately 1 μg/μl with RNase-free water or TE.

Separate labelled RNAs with Dynabeads:
1. Resuspend the beads in the vial (i.e., vortex for >30 sec, or tilt and rotate for 5 min). 
2. Transfer 100uL of beads to a tube for each sample. 
3. Add an equal volume (or at least 1 mL) of 1x B&W Buffer and resuspend. 
4. Place the tube on a magnet for 1 min and discard the supernatant. 
5. Remove the tube from the magnet and resuspend the washed beads in 100uL of 1x B&W Buffer. 
6. Repeat steps 4–5 twice, for a total of 3 washes.
7. Wash the beads twice in 100uL Solution A (DEPC-treated* 0.1 M NaOH, DEPC-treated 0.05 M NaCl)  for 2 min. 
8. Wash the beads twice in 100uL Solution B (DEPC-treated* 0.1 M NaCl). 
9. Resuspend washed Dynabeads™ magnetic beads in 200uL 2X B&W Buffer. 
10. Add 200uL of biotinylated RNA (in distilled water). Optimal binding occurs when the NaCl concentration is reduced from 2 M to 1 M. 
11. Incubate for 15 min at room temperature using gentle rotation.  
12. Separate the biotinylated RNA coated beads with a magnet for 2–3 min. Keep flow through and 1st wash for unlabeled RNA recovery.
13. Wash the coated beads 2–3 times with 100uL 1X B&W Buffer. 
14. Binding is now complete. Resuspend beads in 50uL 0.1xTE for 10 minutes to elute.
15. Measure RNA concentration with nanodrop
